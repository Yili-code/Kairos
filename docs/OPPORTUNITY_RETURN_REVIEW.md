# Opportunity return review

The owner removed fixed six-hour and NT$300 tool-cost exclusions on 2026-10-26. The system still verifies official eligibility, substantial permitted AI production, all required deliverables, online participation, public-display/IP conditions and deadline feasibility. It never applies, pays, accepts terms or contacts organizers.

## What the numbers mean

The hourly values NT$300, NT$600 and NT$1,000 are illustrative comparison scenarios selected for display, not claims about YiLi's personal hourly wage. For each scenario:

`conditional cost = registration fee + incremental tools + estimated full-process hours * scenario hourly value`

`break-even probability for the listed single award = conditional cost / listed cash award`

This threshold is **not** the probability of winning. It does not describe the chance of any prize when multiple prize tiers exist. A threshold above 100% is preserved, not clamped. Costs are AI estimates; if the work/tool plan is uncertain, the comparison remains conditional. Taxes, transfer charges and noncash benefits are not valued, so no positive expected net return is asserted.

The system currently keeps personal success probability and expected net return unknown. Registration counts are not qualified entries; prize slots divided by signups is not an individual winning probability. A personal forecast needs comparable eligible submissions, judging outcomes and evidence about an actual entry's competitive quality. A contest title or AI topic cannot establish it. The card stays ungraded rather than inventing a probability.

## Monetary evidence and exchange references

A single cash amount/currency must be supported in the selected original quotation. Ambiguous currencies, mixed amounts, total prize pools, unsupported fees, negative values and numeric overflow do not become a single-award threshold. Unknown entry fees prevent a complete cost scenario; unknown prize conversion still permits a cost comparison but no numeric break-even threshold.

USD/TWD comparisons use the Central Bank's documented daily interbank closing reference, not a promised transaction rate: https://data.gov.tw/dataset/7232 and https://cpx.cbc.gov.tw/api/OpenData/FTDOpenData_Day. The exact fixed HTTPS endpoint uses the existing bounded public fetcher, its shared cooldown/cache, and no credentials or paid API. Redirects outside that endpoint are rejected. A reference must be fetched within 24 hours, dated no more than seven calendar days ago, nonfuture, finite and nonconflicting. Its date/source remain visible. Other currencies remain unconverted. A source failure is isolated; it never changes unknown into a profitable recommendation.

## Recovery and verification

The policy is part of the assessment context hash; old fixed-cap assessments cannot be treated as current. Historical rejections are not automatically relabeled eligible. A narrowly reviewed, version-guarded migration may return obsolete-policy records to background checking while retaining attempt history and owner decisions. Source acquisition, qualification, cost comparison and real Telegram delivery are verified separately.

Regression tests cover policy boundaries, grounded currencies, entry fees, illustrative time values, missing costs, stale/future/conflicting references, private redirects, overflow and the distinction between thresholds and probabilities. A read-only integration check retrieved the actual official API and calculated conditional scenarios for three saved real-model reports, with zero additional model calls, production candidate writes or Telegram calls. Those calculations are not live recommendation-delivery evidence.
